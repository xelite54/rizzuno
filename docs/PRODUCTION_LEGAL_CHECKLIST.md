# Production legal review

The current release gates and configuration requirements are consolidated in [REGIONAL_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md](REGIONAL_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md), [RETENTION.md](RETENTION.md), and [SAFETY_ESCALATION.md](SAFETY_ESCALATION.md). Complete those records before release. `npm run launch:check` validates supplied declarations; it does not determine legal sufficiency or configure providers.

Paid billing remains disabled. Follow [PAID_BILLING_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md](PAID_BILLING_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md) before adding any paid mode or displaying paid-contract terms.

Terms and Privacy version `2026-09-25b` require reacceptance. Guidelines changes are incorporated through Terms. Prior acceptance rows remain unchanged. Legal identity/contact, reviewed provider/region disclosures, supported countries, an explicit reviewed U.S. state allowlist, an operator-approved `RECENT_MATCH_REPORT_WINDOW_HOURS`, retention approval, trained safety staffing, and approved under-13/CyberTipline/breach procedures are required production inputs. The operator must explicitly configure whether it relies on §512; registration confirmation and agent details become required only when that reliance is true. `COPYRIGHT_NOTICE_EMAIL` is optional when a separate monitored copyright inbox exists. Optional law/dispute clauses render only with explicit review. No actual registration, reviewed jurisdiction, provider region or operator identity is asserted by the repository.

Before launch, staff and rehearse the appeal queue. Underage appeals require trained safety review; no reviewer may decide an enforcement against their own account. User-facing resolutions must exclude reporter identities, restricted evidence and internal notes. An overturn requires checking for later enforcement before restoring prior state.
