# Safety escalation runbook

The age gate is self-attestation, not verified age. General nudity scores must never be treated as CSAM identification. `/admin` shows whether `SevereContentProvider` is registered in the current process. The interface has no default provider; provider exceptions continue to reject image publication. No specialist hash/CSAM detection is claimed while unconfigured. External provider selection, legal review, integration credentials and validation are separate launch decisions.

## Staffing and urgent response

1. Appoint administrators through `ADMIN_EMAILS` and trained safety reviewers through the separate `SAFETY_REVIEWER_EMAILS` allowlist (reviewers must also be admins). Restrict operator database/Storage credentials accordingly. Record training, coverage, escalation contacts, acknowledgement targets and backup coverage before setting `SAFETY_WORKFLOW_APPROVED=true`.
2. Monitor `moderation.urgent_report`, `moderation.urgent_backlog`, oldest unreviewed report and open investigation age. Alerts are log events, not a pager integration: configure actual delivery and test it. Underage reports are urgent and open an investigation; suspension/review does not close that investigation. The `/admin` pending list includes open investigations.
3. Open the linked `/admin/safety/<report ID>` case. Authorization is rechecked on every evidence access and action. Evidence reads are logged. Review minimal text/history evidence and metadata without unnecessary copies. Report-history entries are allegations, not findings. Never infer age or illegal imagery solely from a general classifier.
4. For a credible age concern, use the temporary-suspension control with reason, explicit confirmation and bounded duration. This blocks access while review continues. Track expiry and renew/escalate through a new reviewed enforcement decision if needed. A suspension is not age verification or a finding of criminal conduct.
5. When preservation is justified, create a hold with a case reference and concrete reason. Holds pause all erasure/retention jobs; user edits preserve restricted prior rows. Assign an owner and review date outside the application. Do not retain screenshots or recordings merely because they might be useful. Automatic screenshot capture is disabled: the reserved `ApprovedReportCapture` interface has no adapter, upload API or public evidence URL. Enabling it requires a separate consent/policy, access, retention, safety and browser-permission review.
6. If there is credible imminent danger, the trained reviewer contacts the appropriate emergency service using verified information and jurisdictional guidance. Do not promise real-time emergency response to users. Record `emergency_escalation`, reasoning and receipt/case reference. Never instruct staff to download, reproduce or circulate suspected illegal imagery.
7. Determine external-reporting obligations with the designated safety/legal lead under applicable law. Record `external_report_required` or `external_report_not_required` and the legal/factual rationale. If required, use the authorized external reporting channel and record `external_report_submitted` plus receipt/reference. The application records the decision; it does not submit reports to authorities automatically. For U.S. operation, counsel must assess applicable NCMEC/CyberTipline duties and evidence-preservation requirements; no generic classifier result supplies that legal assessment.
8. Close the investigation explicitly with rationale. Review whether restrictions continue, which evidence remains justified, whether a hold can be released, and what notice/appeal response is appropriate. Keep confidential evidence out of user-facing reasons and standard privacy exports.

## Evidence boundaries

Reports store server-authoritative participants, room ID (for live reports), category/details and time, one snapshot of the last 20 approved text messages from at most two minutes, and up to 20 prior report/action summaries. Duplicate reports reuse their snapshot. Non-call reports have no invented room/chat evidence. Room text remains memory-only unless reported and is cleared when the room ends. Image bytes/video/audio are not included. Evidence and preservation tables are inaccessible to ordinary users. Storage capture is deliberately not implemented.

## Copyright operations

Monitor the designated legal-notice inbox. Assign a case, check notice completeness, identify the actual content, disable qualifying infringing material, notify the uploader through a reviewed contact process, handle counter-notices and calendar the statutory restoration window where applicable. Maintain repeat-infringer decisions, reversals and court-action receipts. Existing moderator account enforcement can disable profile availability; content-specific takedown/restoration and claimant correspondence require restricted operator handling with a case record, not an invented automated workflow. Register and maintain the agent externally before relying on the U.S. process. See `/copyright` and [REGIONAL_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md](REGIONAL_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md).
