# Safety escalation runbook

The age gate is self-attestation, not verified age. General nudity scores must never be treated as CSAM identification. `/admin` shows whether `SevereContentProvider` is registered in the current process. The interface has no default provider; provider exceptions continue to reject image publication. No specialist hash/CSAM detection is claimed while unconfigured. External provider selection, legal review, integration credentials and validation are separate launch decisions.

## Staffing and urgent response

1. Appoint administrators through `ADMIN_EMAILS` and trained safety reviewers through the separate `SAFETY_REVIEWER_EMAILS` allowlist (reviewers must also be admins). Restrict operator database/Storage credentials accordingly. Record training, coverage, escalation contacts, acknowledgement targets and backup coverage before setting `SAFETY_WORKFLOW_APPROVED=true`.
2. Monitor `moderation.urgent_report`, `moderation.urgent_backlog`, oldest unreviewed report and open investigation age. Alerts are log events, not a pager integration: configure actual delivery and test it. Underage reports are urgent and open an investigation; suspension/review does not close that investigation. The `/admin` pending list includes open investigations.
3. Open the linked `/admin/safety/<report ID>` case. Authorization is rechecked on every evidence access and action. Evidence reads are logged. Review minimal text/history evidence and metadata without unnecessary copies. Report-history entries are allegations, not findings. Never infer age or illegal imagery solely from a general classifier.
4. For a credible age concern, use the temporary-restriction control with reason, explicit confirmation and bounded duration. This blocks product access while review continues. Track expiry and renew/escalate through a new reviewed enforcement decision if needed. A restriction is not age verification or a finding of criminal conduct.
5. When preservation is justified, create a hold with a case reference and concrete reason. Holds pause all erasure/retention jobs; user edits preserve restricted prior rows. Assign an owner and review date outside the application. Do not retain screenshots or recordings merely because they might be useful. Automatic screenshot capture is disabled: the reserved `ApprovedReportCapture` interface has no adapter, upload API or public evidence URL. Enabling it requires a separate consent/policy, access, retention, safety and browser-permission review.
6. If there is credible imminent danger, the trained reviewer contacts the appropriate emergency service using verified information and jurisdictional guidance. Do not promise real-time emergency response to users. Record `emergency_escalation`, reasoning and receipt/case reference. Never instruct staff to download, reproduce or circulate suspected illegal imagery.
7. Determine external-reporting obligations with the designated safety/legal lead under applicable law. An ordinary underage report must not be treated as a CyberTipline trigger. For a U.S. case, an authorized reviewer uses the dedicated CyberTipline form to record one of `not_required`, `manual_report_required`, or `manual_report_submitted`, with case reference and rationale. The application records the human decision; it never decides legal coverage or submits to NCMEC. A submitted decision requires the manual submission time, CyberTipline receipt/reference and preservation expiration. No generic classifier result supplies that assessment.
8. Recording `manual_report_submitted` activates scoped preservation of existing report, evidence/history, linked moderation/safety context and match-session material. Expiration must be at least one year after the recorded submission and cannot be shortened while active. A longer global legal hold continues to control. Access is restricted and logged. Do not make a recording, screenshot, or video archive for preservation.
9. If the account is confirmed underage, apply permanent removal through the audited moderation action. If the concern is not confirmed, remove the temporary restriction where no later enforcement prevents restoration.
10. Direct incorrectly flagged users to `/appeals`. The appeal references the recorded enforcement; the reviewer records a user-facing outcome without reporter identities, restricted evidence or internal notes. Underage appeals require a trained safety reviewer and cannot be self-reviewed.
11. Close the investigation explicitly with rationale. Review whether restrictions continue, which evidence remains justified, whether a hold can be released, and what notice/appeal response is appropriate.

## Possible under-13 account

This is a distinct safety/privacy escalation, not a CyberTipline trigger and not an automated legal conclusion.

1. Record `under13_review_opened` with a stable case reference; keep the urgent investigation open and use only the minimum existing report context needed for review.
2. Apply a temporary restriction through the audited enforcement control when the concern is credible and access should stop during review. Do not collect ID documents, contact family members, or seek additional child data through an improvised process.
3. Record `under13_privacy_review_referred` and send the case through the operator-approved privacy/safety procedure. That procedure must identify trained owners, access limits, deletion review, emergency escalation and any required external advice before `UNDER13_RESPONSE_PROCEDURE_APPROVED=true` is set.
4. If the concern is not confirmed, record `under13_not_confirmed`; review whether the restriction can be reversed and then close the investigation separately. If the account is confirmed ineligible, apply the actual permanent enforcement action, record `under13_account_removed`, and review ordinary-data deletion through the existing privacy-erasure workflow.
5. Preserve only records justified by a documented safety/security need, active CyberTipline preservation, or legal hold. Record what was retained and why. Do not treat this flow as proof of age, a COPPA determination, or permission to create recordings, screenshots, or new evidence.
6. Provide the existing appeal route for an incorrect restriction or removal. Keep restricted notes, evidence and reporter identities out of the user-facing result.

## Evidence boundaries

Reports store server-authoritative participants, room ID (for live or recent-session reports), category/details and time, one snapshot of the last 20 approved text messages from at most two minutes when still available, and up to 20 prior report/action summaries. Duplicate reports reuse their snapshot. Recent-session authorization comes from the private match ledger, never browser history. A post-match report normally has no chat snapshot because room text is cleared when the room ends. Image bytes/video/audio are not included. Evidence and preservation tables are inaccessible to ordinary users. The schema can associate an approved evidence object with a report, but automatic browser capture and evidence upload remain disabled.

## Copyright operations

Monitor the designated legal-notice inbox. Assign a case, check notice completeness, identify the actual content, disable qualifying infringing material, notify the uploader through a reviewed contact process, handle counter-notices and calendar the statutory restoration window where applicable. Maintain repeat-infringer decisions, reversals and court-action receipts. Existing moderator account enforcement can disable profile availability; content-specific takedown/restoration and claimant correspondence require restricted operator handling with a case record, not an invented automated workflow. If the operator chooses to rely on 17 U.S.C. §512, register and maintain the agent externally before setting `DMCA_512_RELIANCE=true`. See `/copyright` and [REGIONAL_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md](REGIONAL_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md).
